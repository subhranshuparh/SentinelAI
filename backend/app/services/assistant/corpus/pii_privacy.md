SentinelAI inspects text as you type or paste to intercept sensitive personal identifiers like Aadhaar numbers, PAN identifiers, credit card numbers, and API keys. Using Luhn and Verhoeff checksum validation, high-confidence identifiers are masked locally (e.g. XXXX XXXX 9014) before they leave your browser.
