tags: [qr, upi, payment, scam]
summary: How QR code scams work and how SentinelAI detects malicious payment requests.
---

QR code scams trick victims into scanning codes that initiate money transfers out of their account. A genuine payment QR code for receiving money does not require you to enter your UPI PIN. When a code asks for a PIN or specifies a debit amount, you are sending money, not receiving it. SentinelAI decodes QR images locally and flags payment links that debit funds when you expected a credit.
